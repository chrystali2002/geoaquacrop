Short Experimental Description
1. Control (control_2015)

Purpose: Baseline maize growth under observed 2015 weather conditions.

Treatment:

No weather modification.
Uses original AgERA5 weather.

Result:

Yield = 14.75 t ha⁻¹
Yield gap ≈ 0.3%
No detectable water limitation.
Serves as the reference condition.
2. Early Recovery (early_recovery_2015_v2)

Purpose: Simulate early-season drought followed by recovery prior to reproductive development.

Treatment:

Vegetative rainfall reduced by 85%.
Normal rainfall resumes at reproductive onset.

Result:

Yield = 13.58 t ha⁻¹
Yield gap = 7.1%
Root-zone water partially recovers.
Transpiration largely recovers.
Yield remains at ≈92% of control.

Interpretation:

Recovery of soil water availability supports recovery of transpiration and yield.
3. Late Stress (late_stress_2015_v5)

Purpose: Simulate drought concentrated during grain filling.

Treatment:

Vegetative rainfall reduced moderately.
Reproductive rainfall completely removed.

Result:

Yield = 8.36 t ha⁻¹
Yield gap = 26.7%
Root-zone water declines substantially after reproductive onset.
Transpiration becomes strongly constrained.

Interpretation:

Yield formation is highly sensitive to water limitation during grain filling.
4. Dual Stress (dual_stress_2015_v4)

Purpose: Simulate persistent drought spanning both vegetative and reproductive growth.

Treatment:

Vegetative rainfall reduced strongly.
Reproductive rainfall removed.

Result:

Yield = 8.08 t ha⁻¹
Yield gap = 27.2%
Sustained transpiration limitation.
Lowest biomass accumulation.

Interpretation:

Combined early and late water limitation produces the largest overall stress burden.
